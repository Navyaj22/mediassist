# Dental and Vision Benefits Guide (synthetic)

This document is synthetic sample data created for a demo. It does not describe a real plan.

## Dental coverage

Two routine dental cleanings and exams per year are covered at 100 percent when you visit an in-network dentist. Dental X-rays are covered once every 12 months.

Fillings and simple extractions are covered at 80 percent after a $50 annual dental deductible. Crowns, bridges and dentures are covered at 50 percent, up to an annual dental maximum of $1,500 per person.

Orthodontic care for children under 19, such as braces, is covered at 50 percent up to a lifetime maximum of $2,000.

## Vision coverage

One eye exam per year is covered with a $10 copay. The plan pays up to $150 toward frames every 24 months. Standard lenses are covered after a $25 copay.

If you choose contact lenses instead of glasses, the plan pays up to $120 per year toward contact lenses and the fitting fee.

## Waiting periods

There is no waiting period for cleanings, exams or vision benefits. Major dental work such as crowns and bridges has a 6-month waiting period from your coverage start date.

## How to find an in-network provider

Use the provider directory in the member portal or call the number on the back of your member ID card. Out-of-network dental care is covered at 50 percent of the plan's allowed amount, and you may be billed for any difference.
