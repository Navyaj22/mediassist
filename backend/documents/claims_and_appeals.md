# Claims and Appeals Guide (synthetic)

This document is synthetic sample data created for a demo.

## How claims are filed

In-network providers file claims for you. If you receive care from an out-of-network provider, you may need to submit a claim form with an itemized bill within 180 days of the date of service.

## Claim decisions

The plan decides most claims within 30 days of receiving all required information. You will receive an Explanation of Benefits that shows what was billed, what the plan paid, and what you owe.

## Appealing a denied claim

If a claim is denied, you can file an appeal within 180 days of receiving the denial notice. Send a written appeal that includes your member ID, the claim number, and any supporting documents such as a letter from your doctor.

The plan will respond to a standard appeal within 30 days. If your situation is urgent, you can request an expedited appeal, and the plan will respond within 72 hours.

## External review

If your internal appeal is denied, you can ask for an independent external review. The decision of the external reviewer is final and binding on the plan.
