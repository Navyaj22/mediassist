# Sample Health Plan - Summary of Benefits (synthetic)

This document is synthetic sample data created for a demo. It does not describe a real plan.

## Costs you pay

The annual deductible is $1,500 for an individual and $3,000 for a family. The deductible is the amount you pay for covered services before the plan starts to pay. Preventive care is not subject to the deductible.

The annual out-of-pocket maximum is $5,000 for an individual and $10,000 for a family. After you reach this limit, the plan pays 100 percent of covered services for the rest of the plan year.

## Visit copays

A primary care visit has a $25 copay. A specialist visit has a $50 copay. An urgent care visit has a $75 copay. An emergency room visit has a $300 copay, which is waived if you are admitted to the hospital.

## Preventive care

Annual wellness exams, standard vaccinations and recommended screenings are covered at 100 percent when you use an in-network provider.

## Network

In-network providers have agreed to discounted rates. Out-of-network care is covered at 60 percent after the deductible, and you may be billed for the difference between the provider's charge and the plan's allowed amount.
