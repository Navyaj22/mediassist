# Prescription Drug Coverage (synthetic)

This document is synthetic sample data created for a demo.

## Drug tiers

Tier 1 drugs are generic medications with a $10 copay for a 30-day supply. Tier 2 drugs are preferred brand-name medications with a $40 copay. Tier 3 drugs are non-preferred brand-name medications with a $80 copay. Tier 4 specialty drugs cost 25 percent of the drug price, up to a maximum of $250 per fill.

## Mail order

You can get a 90-day supply of maintenance medications through the mail-order pharmacy for the price of two 30-day copays.

## Prior authorization

Some medications need prior authorization, which means your doctor must get approval from the plan before the drug is covered. Your doctor submits the request, and the plan responds within 72 hours.

## Step therapy

For certain drugs the plan requires you to try a lower-cost alternative first. If the alternative does not work for you, your doctor can request an exception.
